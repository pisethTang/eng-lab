# Description (verbatim)


AI Gateways are becoming the control plane for enterprise LLM usage. This talk explains what they are, why they matter (cost control, security, governance, multi-model routing), when to choose open-source or enterprise options, when self-managing makes sense, and how the AI Gateway offerings from AWS, Azure and Databricks compare.

Speaker:
Thomas Dang: is a software engineer and data engineer specialising in AI-driven solutions. He combines strong engineering foundations with a passion for innovation and focuses on building technology that makes a real difference to the community.
